Our Heritage

A Legacy Woven in Gold & Tradition

Founded in 1982, Lagnadeep Studio began as a small textile atelier in Mumbai's Mulund West. Now in its fourth generation, we continue the timeless art of Bandhani — each knot tied by hand, each thread carrying stories of heritage and devotion.

Every piece that leaves our studio is a testament to the delicate balance between honouring centuries-old craftsmanship and embracing the modern bride's vision.

The Art of Bandhani Tie-Dye

Every Lagnadeep Bandhani piece begins with our master artisans in Gujarat tying thousands of minuscule thread knots by hand onto pure silk. Draped in authentic natural vegetable and heritage mineral dyes, the fabric undergoes multiple immersions to create intricate, spellbinding constellations.

Our Story

Four Generations of Timeless Craftsmanship

Est. 1982

From a Small Atelier to a Legacy

In the bustling lanes of Mulund West, Mumbai, our founder began with a simple vision — to preserve the dying art of Bandhani while creating garments worthy of life's most precious moments.

Four decades and four generations later, Lagnadeep Studio has dressed thousands of brides across the world.

Our Journey

Milestones

The Beginning
